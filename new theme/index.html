Ras Dwivedi

I am passionate about advancing digital trust through blockchain technology and cryptographic solutions. My work focuses on developing secure, transparent, and reliable systems that address real-world challenges. I've contributed to key areas like atomic swaps, privacy-preserving data sharing, self-sovereign identity (SSI), and blockchain applications in e-governance.

In my role as Senior Research Engineer in the National Blockchain Project, I played a pivotal part in bringing blockchain technology from the research lab to real-world applications. I led the deployment of a blockchain-based land registration system in Karnataka, ensuring that the solution was both secure and scalable for state-wide use. Additionally, I spearheaded the launch of an SSI wallet, empowering users to manage their digital identities securely. My work also included the tokenization of Transferable Development Rights for the Kanpur Development Authority, showcasing the versatility of blockchain in urban planning. My focus has always been on translating cutting-edge blockchain research into practical solutions that address real-world challenges, driving innovation with tangible impact.
